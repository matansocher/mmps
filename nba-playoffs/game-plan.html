✅ Locked Decisions DONE

Agreed during brainstorm — these shape the whole build.

Where does it live?
New Vite + React mini-app → apps/nba-playoffs-web (matches coach-web / chatbot-web / expenses-web conventions).
Backend?
Client-only. Deterministic daily seed from the date; scores & streaks in localStorage. No server, no auth for MVP.
How do the modes relate?
The Daily Challenge = that day's Bracket Builder (one shareable score everyone compares). Who Advanced? is a separate endless quick-play practice mode.
How does bracket picking work?
Truth-fill: every one of the 15 series always shows the real teams that actually met; you pick each winner independently. Fair, no dead bracket, all series count.
Is the year shown?
Yes — it's a history/recall game. Seeing "2016 Playoffs" lets fans recall what happened. (Mystery Mode = a later toggle.)

🎮 MVP Modes

1 · Daily Challenge — Bracket Builder IN SCOPE

One year per day, identical for everyone worldwide (date-seeded). Fill the whole bracket, submit once, get a shareable score. The viral core.

  1. Intro card: "2016 NBA Playoffs" + a Start button. Shows your streak 🔥 and whether you've played today.
  2. Play (round-by-round stepper): First Round (8 series) → Conf Semifinals (4) → Conf Finals (2) → Finals (1). Each series is a card showing both real seeded teams; tap the winner. Progress bar on top. One-thumb, fast — no cramped bracket on mobile.
  3. Submit → animated reveal: each pick flips green (right) / red (wrong), round by round, with fun facts on big upsets.
  4. Score & share: round-weighted score /27, streak update, and a spoiler-free emoji share card.

2 · Who Advanced? — Endless Quick Play REMOVED

This mode was built (with 0–4 exact-score guessing) but later removed at the user's request. The game now ships with the Daily Challenge / Bracket Builder only. Kept here for history.

  • Card showed two seeded teams + the year (e.g. "1999 · ⑧ Knicks vs ① Heat — who advanced?").
  • Tap or swipe the winner → instant green/red + a one-line fact.
  • Chased a best streak (hi-score loop). A miss broke the streak but you kept going.
  • Weighted toward juicy upsets (189 in the data) to keep it surprising.

🏆 Scoring & Retention

Bracket (Daily)

  • First Round pick = 1 pt
  • Conf Semifinal = 2 pts
  • Conf Final = 3 pts
  • Finals = 5 pts
  • Perfect bracket = bonus 🎯
  • Max = 27 pts

Streaks & loops

  • Daily streak 🔥 (miss a day = reset)
  • Who Advanced? best-streak hi-score
  • localStorage profile: history, bests, played-today
  • Badges (later): Bracket Master, Upset Oracle

📱 UX Principles

  • One-thumb, fast: tap/swipe only, no typing. Round-by-round card stepper beats a tiny bracket on phones.
  • Instant, satisfying feedback: animated green/red reveal + fun facts turn every answer into a micro-lesson.
  • Sub-3-minute daily: respect time, maximize "just one more."
  • Iconic reveal: after submit, show the filled bracket visual — screenshot-worthy and shareable.
  • No dead ends: wrong pick → learn something → keep flowing.
