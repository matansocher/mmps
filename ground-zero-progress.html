COMPLETED EXPANSION

What changed

IMPLEMENTED

90-degree guard vision

Guards see forward plus the 45-degree space on both sides. Walls and solid props block individual lines of sight, creating a believable cone rather than three simple rays.

IMPLEMENTED

Standing sentries

Some guards stay in place and rotate through configured directions, pausing to scan left, center, and right. Their timing becomes part of the puzzle.

IMPLEMENTED

Larger, denser floors

All five missions are now handcrafted 25×17 facilities with denser walls, rooms, corridors, hiding locations, alternate routes, and path validation.

IMPLEMENTED

Procedural SVG/CSS art

Directional operatives and guards, objective case, locked and open extraction doors, and hiding lockers are generated as scalable SVG/CSS sprites with no external image dependency.

IMPLEMENTED

Suspicion and noise

Detection now raises a readable awareness meter instead of causing an immediate distant alarm. Movement creates short-lived noise pulses that attract guards within a floor-specific radius.

IMPLEMENTED

Investigation and search AI

Guards pathfind to sound or last-seen positions, search the area, then return to their patrol route or sentry post. Their color communicates routine, investigation, search, and return states.

IMPLEMENTED

Smooth interpolated movement

Player, guard, and camera positions now animate between deterministic grid ticks. Quick taps are buffered, key releases stop immediately, and board taps no longer snap the camera.

IMPLEMENTED

Interactive Lobby routes

The first mission now introduces a collectible access card, a sealed door that opens after authorization, and paired vents that instantly create alternate infiltration routes.

IMPLEMENTED ARCHITECTURE

How the expansion works

IMPLEMENTED

Use angular field-of-view with occlusion

Candidate tiles are included when their angle is within 45 degrees of the guard's facing direction. Each candidate then receives a grid line-of-sight check, such as Bresenham traversal, so walls can block one side of the cone without blocking the other.

IMPLEMENTED

Add guard behavior types

Guards use explicit patrol and sentry routines plus shared investigate, search, and return modes. Breadth-first pathfinding routes alerted guards around walls without changing the deterministic grid simulation.

IMPLEMENTED

Render a viewport, not the whole map

The current renderer fits the entire floor onto the Canvas. Larger floors would make every tile tiny. A camera should follow the player while rendering a fixed-size viewport, with a minimap or room map for orientation.

IMPLEMENTED

Keep simulation and presentation separate

Game rules remain grid-based and deterministic. Sprites, lighting, animation, camera movement, and particles only visualize that state, preserving reliable collision and detection tests.
